Made for the moments
that matter.

A worship presentation tool should feel natural. Caleb Media focuses on the song workflow, from preparation to the first lyric on screen.

Your song library

Keep worship songs organized in one local library. Find what you need and keep song content ready for the next service.

Section-based lyrics

Structure songs into verses, choruses, bridges, tags, outros, and custom sections. Reorder the flow to match the song.

Service setlists

Build the worship sequence ahead of time. Keep songs in order and move through the service without losing your place.

Two-screen presentation

Operate from your main display while lyrics appear on the external projector. Keep the controls with the operator.

Preview before live

Prepare the next section while the current lyric stays on screen. Preview and Live are separate so every transition is intentional.

Ready when you are

Designed for a dependable local workflow. Your core presentation setup does not rely on a cloud account or constant internet access.
